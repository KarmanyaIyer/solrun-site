Solrun Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 1, 2026

Some US state laws treat information about your physical activity as consumer health data. In Solrun, that is your walking and running data (GPS location, routes, distances, steps, pace, and run times) and your setup answers about how active you are and your goals.

  • Solrun processes this data only on your iPhone, to measure your runs and suggest your rate. It is not sent to ClankerMode or to our service providers, and we do not sell or share it.
  • If usage statistics are on, PostHog receives a note that a run was started, finished, or discarded, how it was measured (GPS, step counting, or neither), and whether it was recovered after the app closed. These notes carry your support ID. It never receives distance, steps, pace, duration, route, location, or minutes earned. You can turn usage statistics off in Solrun's settings at any time.
  • Deleting the app deletes the walking and running data stored on your iPhone. It does not delete the run notes PostHog already received. To have those deleted, or for any other question or request about this data, email support@clankermode.com with the support ID shown in Solrun's settings.
